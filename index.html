Probabilistic ML and Bayesian systems

Finding structure in places that resist it.

Aleatoric Lab builds probabilistic ML and Bayesian systems. The most useful models do not just give you an answer. They tell you how much to trust it.

The name comes from the Latin alea, the roll of a die. Aleatoric uncertainty is the randomness that stays inside a system however much data you collect. We build for the places where being honest about that is a necessity.
